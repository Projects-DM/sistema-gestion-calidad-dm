# Sprint Definition Standard

Every Sprint must contain:

## Business Objective

What problem are we solving?

---

## Expected Result

What should the user obtain?

---

## Scope

Exactly what is included.

---

## Out of Scope

Exactly what is NOT included.

---

## Reuse Inventory

List all existing components that will be reused.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

Functional validation checklist.

---

## Certification

Only after manual validation.
